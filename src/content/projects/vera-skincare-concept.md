### Overview

VÉRA is an independent skincare website concept exploring how premium imagery, refined typography, and carefully structured product presentation can create a distinctive online shopping experience.

### The design direction

The concept uses a dark botanical palette, elegant serif typography, warm gold accents, and large-scale lifestyle imagery.

The hero introduces the skincare identity through expressive imagery and the headline "Your Skin, Restored."

A featured-product treatment and prominent shopping calls to action establish the visual direction of the storefront.

### What the concept explores

The design includes a hero section, curated bestseller products, product cards, and an editorial brand-storytelling section.

The layout balances commercial product discovery with a more atmospheric, beauty-focused presentation.

The concept demonstrates the use of visual hierarchy, photography, typography, and composition to build a coherent e-commerce design.

### Outcome

A completed independent website design concept demonstrating a premium skincare storefront direction.

### Important classification

This is an independent design concept, not a commissioned client project.
