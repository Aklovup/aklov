### Overview

A role-based operations portal bringing quotation, order management, quality control, shipping, and financial processes into a connected workflow.

### The challenge

Xans had several operational activities to manage across the order lifecycle, from preparing quotations and handling orders to quality checks, shipping, and financial records.

The application needed to bring these activities into a more connected working environment while supporting the different responsibilities of the people involved.

### What I built

I developed a custom operations portal using Zoho Creator, with role-based access and workflows supporting different stages of business operations.

The application covered quotation management, order processing, quality control logging, shipping activities, and financial tracking, including receivables and payables.

I also implemented interactive forms, action-based interfaces, and transaction document generation to help users carry out tasks within the application.

### The outcome

The completed portal provides a centralized environment for managing interconnected operational activities, giving different users access to the workflows and information relevant to their responsibilities.

### Technologies

Zoho Creator, Deluge, custom forms, role-based workflows, document generation.
