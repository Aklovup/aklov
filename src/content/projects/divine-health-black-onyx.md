### Overview

A multi-brand shopping experience that gives Divine Health & Wellness and Black Onyx their own visual identities while sharing the underlying commerce infrastructure.

### The challenge

Divine Health & Wellness needed an online presence that could accommodate different product identities without creating disconnected shopping systems.

Alongside its main wellness brand, the business introduced Black Onyx as a distinct product line. Each needed an appropriate storefront experience, but customers still needed access to shared shopping, account, and checkout functionality.

The challenge was to create that separation within the capabilities of Zoho Commerce while maintaining a consistent purchasing experience.

### What I built

I worked across Zoho Sites and Zoho Commerce to develop the website and shopping experience.

The main Divine Health & Wellness website was built in Zoho Sites, with its commerce functionality handled through Zoho Commerce.

Within the commerce environment, I implemented a category-based approach to distinguish the Black Onyx product line from the main Divine Health & Wellness shopping experience.

Using custom CSS and JavaScript, I developed different visual treatments and navigation experiences while keeping both connected to the same underlying commerce infrastructure.

This allowed the storefronts to maintain separate brand presentations while sharing core shopping functionality, including the cart, checkout, and customer account environment.

### The outcome

The completed implementation supports two distinct customer-facing brand experiences within one connected commerce infrastructure.

It demonstrates how a commerce platform can be customized to accommodate more complex branding and shopping requirements.

### Technologies

Zoho Sites, Zoho Commerce, CSS, JavaScript.
