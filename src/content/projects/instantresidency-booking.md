### Overview

A consultation-booking automation connecting conversational AI with calendar availability, appointment management, customer records, and confirmations.

### The challenge

An immigration and residency consultancy needed a structured way to handle consultation-booking requests.

Scheduling required more than collecting a preferred date. The workflow needed to interpret customer requests, check appointment availability, manage bookings, maintain lead information, and communicate confirmations.

These activities also needed to work together across the business's existing applications.

### What I built

I developed an AI-powered consultation booking workflow using n8n and an OpenAI-powered agent with conversational memory.

The workflow integrated Google Calendar to check availability, create appointments, and cancel bookings where required.

I connected Airtable for lead and booking information, and Gmail for confirmation messages.

The system also supported structured outputs for SMS and WhatsApp communication, along with date and time interpretation to help translate conversational booking requests into scheduling actions.

### The outcome

The completed solution connects consultation conversations with the underlying scheduling and customer-record workflows.

It provides a structured way to manage availability checks, appointment actions, customer information, and confirmation communications within one automation system.

### Technologies

n8n, OpenAI, Google Calendar, Airtable, Gmail, conversational memory.
