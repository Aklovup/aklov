### Overview

An intelligent customer-support workflow connecting conversational AI with tour knowledge, pickup information, reservation resources, and contextual search.

### The challenge

A tour business needed a way to handle recurring customer questions about its travel experiences.

These questions could involve tour details, hotel pickup arrangements, reservation links, and other practical information customers need before or after booking.

The support experience needed access to relevant business information rather than relying entirely on generic AI-generated responses.

### What I built

I developed an AI-powered customer support workflow using n8n, integrating an OpenAI language model with contextual memory and a Qdrant vector database.

The agent was designed to retrieve relevant tour information and respond to customer inquiries using available knowledge resources.

Its capabilities included answering frequently asked questions, providing tour and reservation information, retrieving hotel pickup details, sharing booking links, and assisting with exchange-rate-related inquiries.

I also structured the response formatting and conversational workflow to support clear, useful interactions.

### The outcome

The completed solution provides a conversational support workflow that retrieves relevant travel information and responds to common customer inquiries through a connected AI system.

It demonstrates how AI, knowledge retrieval, and workflow automation can be combined to support customer service operations.

### Technologies

n8n, OpenAI, Qdrant, vector search, conversational memory.
