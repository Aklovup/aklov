### Overview

A members-only Zoho Commerce experience combining controlled access, customer onboarding, and referral and loyalty management.

### The challenge

Wound Concierge needed a private online shopping environment where access to the storefront could be managed through an approval process.

Beyond the shopping experience, the business also needed a way to coordinate customer information, referral relationships, and loyalty rewards across its Zoho applications.

The challenge was to connect these activities without treating the storefront, customer records, and rewards program as completely separate processes.

### What I built

I developed and customized a members-only storefront using Zoho Commerce, with attention to the shopping experience, product presentation, and customer-facing pages.

I also worked on the supporting customer-access process, using Zoho Forms for registration and approval-related information and connecting customer records with Zoho Inventory.

For referral and loyalty management, I configured workflows involving Zoho Thrive, including referral relationships and reward-point functionality.

The overall process combined application integrations, configured workflows, and defined manual administrative steps where necessary.

I also prepared an internal standard operating procedure to document how the relevant processes should be managed.

### The outcome

The completed project brings together a controlled commerce environment and supporting customer-management processes.

The setup provides a structured way to manage approved customers, maintain connected records, and operate referral and loyalty activities.

### Technologies

Zoho Commerce, Zoho Forms, Zoho Inventory, Zoho Thrive, connected Zoho workflows.
