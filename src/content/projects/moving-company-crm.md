### Overview

A Zoho CRM system designed around the operational needs of a moving and storage business, from incoming leads through job coordination and completion.

### The challenge

The moving company needed a more structured way to manage the journey from an incoming customer inquiry to a scheduled and completed moving job.

Its requirements went beyond conventional sales pipeline tracking.

The system needed to account for job scheduling, morning and afternoon bookings, full-day moves, crew assignments, and coordination between office staff and field workers.

The business also needed its CRM processes to work alongside customer intake, communication, and calendar tools.

### What I built

I assessed the existing Zoho CRM setup, identified operational gaps, and implemented a more structured approach to managing the lead-to-job process.

The work covered CRM organization, customer and job information, booking workflows, scheduling requirements, and crew-coordination processes.

The implementation was guided by the company's operational structure, including its booking arrangements and the different responsibilities involved in managing moving jobs.

I also addressed the supporting integration requirements involving Gravity Forms, RingCentral, Gmail, and Google Calendar as part of the overall CRM solution.

### The outcome

The completed CRM project provides a more structured foundation for managing customer inquiries, coordinating bookings, organizing job information, and supporting moving operations.

The solution was designed around the business's actual service-delivery workflow rather than a generic sales pipeline.

### Technologies

Zoho CRM, Gravity Forms, RingCentral, Gmail, Google Calendar.
