### Overview

A custom ordering portal bringing product browsing, cart management, Stripe checkout, and inventory synchronization together within Zoho Creator.

### The challenge

Impex Automotive needed a dedicated business ordering experience that could bring customers through product selection and checkout while remaining connected to its underlying inventory system.

The application needed to support the shopping journey while allowing greater control over how the interface and business processes were presented.

### What I built

I developed a branded B2B ordering portal using Zoho Creator, combining custom interface development with the application's underlying business logic.

The solution included product browsing, a shopping cart, and Stripe checkout integrated into the ordering experience. Inventory synchronization connected the customer-facing application with product availability information.

I used custom HTML, CSS, Zoho Creator widgets, and Deluge to create the interface and support its functionality.

### The outcome

The completed application brings the customer's ordering journey and inventory information into one connected environment, with an interface tailored to the business.

### Technologies

Zoho Creator, Deluge, HTML, CSS, custom widgets, Stripe.
