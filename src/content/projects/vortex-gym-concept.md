### Overview

Vortex Gym is an independent fitness website concept exploring a bold visual approach to presenting training programs, gym facilities, and membership-oriented calls to action.

### The design direction

The concept uses a predominantly black and charcoal palette with vivid lime-green accents.

Large, condensed typography creates a strong visual presence, while gym photography reinforces the athletic identity.

The opening section introduces the brand through the headline "Forge Your Best Self," supported by prominent calls to action.

### What the concept explores

The design includes a hero section, featured training programs, visual fitness categories, and a gym-benefits section.

Program cards use large photography and strong typography to distinguish different training offerings.

The overall composition emphasizes clarity, energy, and direct navigation toward membership-related actions.

### Outcome

A completed independent website design concept demonstrating a distinctive fitness brand direction.

### Important classification

This is an independent design concept, not a commissioned client project.
