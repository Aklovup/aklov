### Overview

Custom storefront development for JAYCOR, refining product discovery and shopping interfaces within Zoho Commerce.

### The challenge

JAYCOR supplies specialist products across industrial connectivity, enterprise IT and networking, and broadcast and audiovisual equipment.

Its online store needed to present a broad technical product catalog through a shopping experience that felt consistent with the company's brand.

Working within Zoho Commerce meant adapting platform-generated pages rather than designing every interface entirely from scratch.

### What I built

I customized the Zoho Commerce storefront using CSS and JavaScript, focusing on the presentation and behavior of key shopping pages.

The work included category listings, individual product pages, cart interfaces, and other commerce-generated elements.

These customizations helped establish a more consistent visual experience across different parts of the store while retaining the underlying Zoho Commerce functionality.

The implementation required working with the platform's existing page structures and styling constraints.

### The outcome

The completed customization provides a more tailored shopping interface aligned with JAYCOR's brand and specialist product catalog.

The project demonstrates practical front-end development within an established commerce platform.

### Technologies

Zoho Commerce, CSS, JavaScript.
