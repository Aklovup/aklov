Our Philosophy

What’s Inside

Ingredients that
earn their place

No filler. No fragrance. No compromise. Every ingredient in a VÉRA formula is chosen for a specific, proven function — and declared fully on every label.

Patagonia, Chile
Cold-Pressed Rosehip

Rich in trans-retinoic acid and essential fatty acids — naturally accelerates cell turnover and visibly softens post-blemish marks.

Biofermentation · EU Lab
Ceramide Complex III

Replenishes the lipid matrix of the stratum corneum, locking in moisture and fortifying the skin’s natural defence against environmental aggressors.

Psoralea Corylifolia · India
Bakuchiol

Plant-derived retinol alternative with equivalent efficacy at 0.5% and zero irritation risk — safe for pregnancy, reactive, and rosacea-prone skin.
